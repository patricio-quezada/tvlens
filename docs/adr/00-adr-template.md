# NN. Short title of the decision

**One or two sentences saying what was decided and why, before anything else.** Someone who hit
a reference to this record in the code should be able to stop reading here and still know the
answer. Everything below is for the reader who wants to know how it was arrived at.

## Context
What was the situation, and what problem or need drove this? Open on the moment the problem
showed up, not on a description of the system. Tell the story of how it got here in plain
language, the facts and constraints, not the decision yet.

## Decision
What the system does now, explained so someone still learning could follow it. Define any term
the first time it appears and skip insider shorthand. Include the rule or the shape of the
change, and the alternatives considered with why they lost.

## After Action Review
Did it get the result it wanted? If yes, what shows it. If not, what happened and how to get
better. Note where it is tested and any open item to revisit later.

---

Write it to be read. Short paragraphs, two to four sentences. Vary the sentence length. Prefer a
picture to an abstraction: "paying full price for an answer it already had" lands where "a
computation whose answer does not depend on the viewer" does not. If a sentence is a list, make
it a list. Name who acts. First person for judgements, the system for what the system does, and
no second person outside quoted product copy.
